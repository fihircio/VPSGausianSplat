# Pilot Pricing Hypotheses — Internal Draft

**Status: not an approved rate card or customer offer.** Use the bounded pilot in [pilot_one_pager.md](pilot_one_pager.md) as the only current offer. Do not present Venue or Network packages, feature entitlements, hosting commitments, or service levels as available products.

## Current pricing hypothesis

For discovery conversations only, test whether buyers see value in a fixed-scope, supported evaluation:

| Initial scope hypothesis | Indicative range |
|---|---:|
| One small zone with one demo path | USD 8,000–15,000 |
| More complex area or integration | USD 20,000–40,000, only after engineering and delivery scoping |

These figures are unvalidated. They are not a quote, commitment, or evidence of willingness to pay. Set a fixed fee only after confirming area, data path, integration effort, deployment, support, customer responsibilities, and payment terms in a written proposal.

## Not currently offered as packages

The following items from earlier tier drafts are future hypotheses and must not be promised as current capabilities:

- Full-building, campus-wide, or multi-site coverage.
- Unlimited maps or anchors.
- Tenant isolation or agency sub-licensing guarantees.
- Hosted cloud service, committed uptime, or response-time SLA.
- Priority support, dedicated account management, or quarterly service reviews.
- Billing, quota enforcement, or self-serve account plans.
- Automatic pilot-to-annual conversion credits.

Revisit these only after implementation, operational readiness, and buyer evidence support a specific offer. Any future service level or data-isolation statement needs technical and legal review before external use.

## Pricing discovery questions

- What budget range is available for a bounded technical evaluation?
- Which pilot outcome would justify that spend?
- Who approves the spend and what procurement steps apply?
- Which site, data, integration, and support costs should be included?
- What would need to be true before an annual or expanded deployment is considered?

Record customer-stated ranges and decisions separately from internal estimates. Do not infer willingness to pay from positive feedback alone.
