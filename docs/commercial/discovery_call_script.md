# Discovery Call Script

## Call Goal

Determine whether the buyer has a real spatial positioning or AR anchoring problem, a reachable pilot scope, and budget authority.

## Opening

We are exploring a small paid pilot to prove whether visual positioning can work reliably in your space. The goal is not a long platform rollout first. It is a scoped validation with a working demo and measured results.

## Qualification Questions

### Problem

- What physical space or route are you trying to improve?
- What breaks today with GPS, QR markers, manual alignment, or existing maps?
- Is the problem navigation, AR content placement, staff workflow, visitor experience, or something else?
- Who experiences the pain most often?

### Current Workflow

- What are you using today?
- Have you tried AR, indoor maps, beacons, QR codes, BLE, UWB, or manual guides?
- What failed or was too expensive?
- What systems would a pilot need to integrate with?

### Pilot Scope

- What is the smallest area where a successful demo would matter?
- Is it indoor, outdoor, or mixed?
- Can we access the site or data?
- Are there lighting, crowd, security, or privacy constraints?
- Who approves capture or data use?

### Success Metrics

- What would make the pilot a clear yes?
- What accuracy is useful for the use case?
- How quickly does localization need to happen?
- How many users/devices would be involved in a demo?
- What would make this not worth continuing?

### Buying Process

- Who owns the budget?
- Is this innovation, operations, marketing, facilities, or IT budget?
- What budget range is realistic for a 4-6 week pilot?
- Who signs off?
- What procurement/security review is needed for a pilot?

## Positioning Points

Use these only when relevant:

- We provide visual positioning and persistent spatial anchors.
- The first pilot is deliberately small and measurable.
- Google 3D can accelerate outdoor synthetic training and demos where permission allows.
- Real deployment claims are validated using real device data in the target space.
- Unity/Web integration is the first developer path.

## Red Flags

- No clear owner.
- No access to site/data.
- Buyer wants full production SLA before pilot.
- Accuracy expectations are unrealistic for the environment.
- No budget even for a small pilot.
- Use case is safety-critical from day one.

## Close

If there is fit:

> The next step is a short pilot scope document. We will define the area, success metrics, timeline, customer responsibilities, and fixed pilot fee.

Ask:

- Who should review the pilot scope?
- What date should we target for a proposal review?
- What would block approval?
