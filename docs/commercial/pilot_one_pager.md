# VPS Spatial Pilot

## Offer

We help venues and facility teams test whether their space can support reliable visual positioning and persistent AR anchoring.

The pilot turns one defined zone, route, floor, or outdoor area into a measurable spatial demo:

```text
map or model the space -> localize a camera frame -> place persistent anchors -> measure reliability
```

## Best Fit

- Museums, galleries, and immersive venues.
- Retail destinations and showrooms.
- Campuses, hospitals, and complex facilities.
- AR agencies building client activations.
- Property developers with physical demo centers.

## Pilot Scope

Included:

- One defined pilot area.
- VPS map or demo map preparation.
- Web portal access.
- Localization API.
- Unity SDK or Navigatus demo integration.
- 1-3 persistent AR anchors.
- Accuracy and reliability report.
- Final recommendation for rollout, expansion, or stop.

Optional:

- Google 3D synthetic data benchmark for outdoor areas.
- Real-device capture support.
- Custom Unity/WebAR integration support.

## Timeline

Typical duration: 4-6 weeks after site/data access.

1. Week 1: scope, site/data review, success metrics.
2. Week 2-3: map/data preparation and demo integration.
3. Week 4: validation and iteration.
4. Week 5-6: final demo, report, and rollout proposal.

## Success Metrics

The pilot is evaluated with measurable technical and commercial gates:

- Localization success rate.
- Median and p95 position error.
- Median rotation error.
- Time to first localization.
- Processing turnaround time.
- Demo acceptance by customer team.
- Rollout feasibility and expected next step.

## Pricing Hypothesis

- Small venue pilot: USD 8k-15k.
- Enterprise or complex facility pilot: USD 20k-40k.

Pilot fee can be partially credited toward an annual rollout if converted within an agreed window.

## Important Boundaries

We do not claim full-building production accuracy from synthetic data alone.

Google 3D data, where permitted, is used for synthetic training, outdoor demo acceleration, and benchmarking. Paid deployment claims are based on real validation in the target environment.
